# Scope decisions

## In scope
- Weekly first-response SLA breach reporting.
- Agent + shift reporting.
- UTC→IST conversion.
- Effective-dated roster matching.
- Migration duplicate handling.
- Within-shift vs carryover diagnostic.
- SLA-credit exposure.
- Optional AI management summary.

## Out of scope for the five-hour MVP
- CSAT trend analysis.
- Refund/replacement economics.
- Customer segmentation.
- Product/lot defect analysis.
- Predictive staffing.
- Automated agent coaching.
- Full root-cause classification of all free-text tickets.

Reason: the client explicitly asked for a weekly agent/shift SLA report, and the data reveals a material roster/queue-coverage issue that is more actionable than a broader scorecard.
